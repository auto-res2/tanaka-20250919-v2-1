# Experiment Strategy
1. Head-to-Head Multi-Objective Benchmarking
   Goal: Show absolute gains on all three axes (helpfulness, calibration, safety) with identical compute.
   Setup:
   • Models: Zephyr-7B fine-tuned with (i) SFT, (ii) DPO, (iii) DiscoPOP, (iv) AdaLoss-LM, (v) RL-blend (best tuned α), and (vi) SafeAdaLoss-LM.
   • Hardware: 1×A100-80GB per run; 3 seeds each (total <36 GPU-hours – comfortably inside resources).
   • Data: Argilla DPO Mix 7 k (same split).
   • Metrics: MT-Bench & AlpacaEval-2.0 (helpfulness), RealToxicityPrompts + HarassmentEval (toxicity), BBQ (bias), WikiText-2 ECE (calibration), wall-clock time.
   Expected read-out:
   • SafeAdaLoss beats best baseline by ≥+1 MT-Bench, ≥−30 % toxicity, ≥−20 % bias while preserving AdaLoss ECE gain and ≤1.3× training time.
   Demonstration: Confirms the core claim that a single, instance-adaptive loss can jointly improve helpfulness, calibration and behavioural safety better than any fixed-weight alternative.

2. Context-Dependent Safety Stress-Test
   Goal: Verify that the hyper-network really adapts penalty weights per instance instead of bluntly suppressing the model.
   Setup:
   • Construct 1 000 two-turn conversations: 500 high-risk (war, hate speech, self-harm) and 500 low-risk (cooking, travel, math). Prompts are balanced for length & complexity.
   • For every sample, log the learned weight simplex (α…ε) emitted by Safe-MLT and compare to a global-λ RL baseline (best tuned λ).
   • Evaluate generations with Detoxify + bias classifier and ask GPT-4 to score helpfulness (0-10).
   Expected read-out:
   • In high-risk prompts, median (δ+ε) weight ≥0.35, toxicity <3 %, helpfulness ≥8.0.
   • In low-risk prompts, median (δ+ε) weight ≤0.10, toxicity still <3 %, helpfulness ≥9.0 – a +1.2 gain over RL-blend which suppresses helpfulness uniformly.
   Demonstration: Visual correlation plots of weight mass vs toxicity/ bias prove SafeAdaLoss learns to “dial safety up or down” on a per-instance basis, achieving both context sensitivity and higher utility.

3. Hyper-Parameter Robustness & Compute Efficiency Study
   Goal: Show that SafeAdaLoss eliminates expensive coefficient sweeps that baselines require.
   Setup:
   • Take RL-blend and DiscoPOP; sweep λ∈{0,0.1,…,1.0} (11 values) → 66 extra fine-tunes.
   • Take SafeAdaLoss single run (default settings).
   • Compare (i) best-achievable F1 on MT-Bench × Safety (toxicity<5 % & bias<0.05) envelope, and (ii) aggregate GPU-hours.
   Expected read-out:
   • SafeAdaLoss lands inside top-3 % of the best tuned baseline’s envelope with 1/11th the runs.
   • Efficiency plot: baseline area under Pareto curve reached at 400 GPU-h vs 36 GPU-h for SafeAdaLoss.
   Demonstration: Empirically validates the “no brittle hyper-parameter sweep” claim and highlights practical savings for smaller labs, underscoring the method’s effectiveness from an operations perspective.
